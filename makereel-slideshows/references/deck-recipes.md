# Deck recipes

Beat orders for common TikTok photo-slideshow archetypes. Adapt slide counts to the content; keep one idea per slide. Hooks shown are patterns, not copy to reuse verbatim.

## Listicle — "N things"

Best for: teach pillar, saves.

1. Hook with the number and the payoff: "5 desk swaps that fixed my back pain".
2–N+1. One item per slide: heading names the item, optional sentence says why it matters.
N+2. Closer: which one to try first, or "save this for your next setup". Soft plug may sit on the strongest item.

## Mistakes — "Stop doing X"

Best for: teach pillar, comments.

1. Mistake callout: "You're washing your face wrong in humid weather".
2–4. One mistake per slide, each with the fix in one sentence.
5. What to do instead, summarized.
6. CTA: "Which one were you doing?"

## Steps — "How to"

Best for: tutorials, product use.

1. Outcome hook: "Meal prep 5 lunches in 45 minutes".
2. What you need (one slide).
3–N. One step per slide, numbered in the heading, action verb first.
N+1. Result slide showing the outcome image.
N+2. CTA: save / follow for part 2.

## Story / transformation

Best for: relate and prove pillars, Avatar-led decks.

1. Before-state or tension hook: "I almost quit my shop in March".
2. Context: what was going wrong.
3. Turning point.
4–5. What changed, concretely.
6. Where things are now (only results the user can substantiate).
7. Lesson + CTA.

## Myth vs fact

Best for: authority in crowded niches.

1. Contrarian hook: "Drinking more water won't fix dry skin".
2–5. Alternate or pair: myth heading, fact sentence with its reason. Keep the visual treatment consistent so viewers learn the pattern.
6. Summary + CTA.

## Comparison — "A vs B"

Best for: decision-stage audiences.

1. Question hook: "Linen or cotton for a Saigon summer?"
2. The criteria (price, feel, care).
3–5. One criterion per slide, using a 2-image split layout.
6. Verdict for each type of person, not a universal winner.
7. CTA: comment their pick.

## POV / relatable

Best for: reach, shares, persona building.

1. POV hook: "POV: it's 3pm and your makeup has left the chat".
2–5. Escalating relatable moments, short text, strong candid images.
6. Twist or small useful tip.
7. Tag-a-friend CTA.

## Soft plug

Best for: the invite pillar. Use sparingly.

1. Problem hook in the audience's words.
2–3. Why the usual fixes fail.
4. What worked, with the product appearing naturally in a real-use image, peer voice.
5. Honest detail (price range, who it is not for).
6. CTA: "questions? ask below". Add the platform's paid-partnership disclosure when the post is sponsored.
