## Example (abbreviated)

A skincare founder wants followers before a serum launch, 3 weeks, 4 carousels per week, English, Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.

- Positioning: for oily-skin 20-somethings in humid cities, the brand's serum is the lightweight hydration step that doesn't clog pores, unlike heavy creams.
- Pillars: teach "humid-weather routine mistakes" (40%, Scratch listicle), relate "POV: your makeup at 3pm" (25%, Social remix of a curated Viral Post), prove "4-week texture diary" using the founder's Avatar (20%, Scratch story), invite "launch countdown" (15%, Scratch soft plug).
- Test: number hooks ("3 mistakes…") vs POV hooks on comparable teach posts; slide count held at 6.
